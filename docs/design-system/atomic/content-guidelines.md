# Atomic Content Guidelines

## Voice
- Calm, direct, actionable.
- Avoid gamified language, streak pressure, or celebratory noise.

## Microcopy rules
- Action labels are verb-first and concise.
- Error messages explain what happened and how to recover.
- Empty states suggest the next planner action (capture, clarify, or commit).

## Planner-first priority
- Primary text emphasizes today priorities and next executable step.
- Insights and companion text is supportive, short, and visually secondary.
